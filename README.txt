HOW TO FINISH THIS SITE
========================

1. Drop your media into these folders using the EXACT filenames below.
   (Case matters on most hosting platforms, so keep PHOTO in caps and .JPG in caps.)

   images/
     cat.gif
     cat3.gif
     cat4.gif
     PHOTO1.JPG ... PHOTO21.JPG

   audio/
     Happy Birthday Meow Meow Cat.mp3
     song1.mp3

   videos/
     video1.mp4

2. Open index.html by double-clicking it to preview locally in your browser.

3. When you're happy with it, follow the "GOING LIVE" instructions Claude gave you
   in the chat to publish it (Netlify Drop is the fastest option).

That's it — no build tools, no installs needed.
